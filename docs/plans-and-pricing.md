# Plans and pricing

## Free
$0 per month. Up to 5 invoices per month, 2 seats, and card payments with a 1% Ledgerly fee on top of the payment processor's fee.

## Pro
$19 per month. Unlimited invoices, recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders, 10 seats and no Ledgerly fee on card payments.

## Business
$49 per month. Everything in Pro, plus unlimited seats, API access and the QuickBooks integration.

## Annual billing
Paying annually gives you two months free: Pro is $190 per year and Business is $490 per year.

## Trial
Pro and Business both come with a 14-day free trial. If you don't add a payment method before the trial ends, the workspace returns to the Free plan.
