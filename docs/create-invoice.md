# Creating and sending an invoice

## Create the invoice
Click New invoice, pick a customer (or add a new one), and add line items with a description, quantity and unit price. Set the issue date and the payment terms; the default terms are Net 30.

## Send it
Click Send to email the invoice. The customer receives a link to view the invoice online and pay it, and a PDF copy is attached. You can also download the PDF and send it yourself.

## Edit after sending
You can edit an invoice until a payment is recorded against it. When you save changes, the customer's online link shows the updated version, and you can choose whether to email them again.
