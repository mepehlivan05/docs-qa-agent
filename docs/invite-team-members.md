# Inviting team members

## Send an invite
Go to Settings > Team and click Invite. Enter the person's email address and choose a role. The invite link expires after 7 days; you can resend it from the same page.

## Roles
- **Owner**: full access, including billing and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one owner.
- **Admin**: everything except billing and deleting the workspace.
- **Member**: can create and send invoices and record payments, but cannot change settings.
- **Viewer**: read-only access to invoices and reports.

## Seat limits
The Free plan includes 2 seats, Pro includes 10 seats and Business has unlimited seats. Viewers do not count toward the seat limit.

## Transfer ownership
The current owner can transfer ownership to an Admin under Settings > Team > Transfer ownership. The previous owner becomes an Admin.
