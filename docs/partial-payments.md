# Partial payments and deposits

## Record a partial payment
Use Record payment and enter less than the full amount. The invoice status changes to Partially paid and the remaining balance is shown on the invoice.

## Request a deposit
When creating an invoice, turn on Request deposit and enter a percentage or a fixed amount. The customer can pay the deposit online first and the balance later.

## Reminders on partial payments
Payment reminders continue for the remaining balance until the invoice is fully paid.
