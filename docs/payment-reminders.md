# Automatic payment reminders

Payment reminders are available on the Pro and Business plans.

## Default schedule
By default, Ledgerly emails the customer 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after the due date if the invoice is still unpaid.

## Customize reminders
Under Settings > Reminders you can change the timing and the wording of each reminder. An invoice can have at most 5 reminders.

## Turn reminders off
You can turn reminders off for the whole workspace, for one customer, or for a single invoice. Reminders stop automatically once the invoice is fully paid.
