# Refunding a payment

## Refund a card payment
Open the invoice, select the payment and click Refund. You can refund the full amount or part of it. Card payments can be refunded within 180 days of the original payment.

## Fees on refunds
Stripe does not return its processing fee when you issue a refund. The Ledgerly 1% fee on the Free plan is also not returned.

## Refund a bank transfer
Ledgerly cannot send bank transfers. Refund the customer from your bank, then record the refund on the invoice so your reports stay accurate.
