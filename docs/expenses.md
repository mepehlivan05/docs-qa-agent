# Tracking expenses

## Add an expense
Click Expenses > New expense and enter the amount, date, category and vendor. You can mark an expense as billable to add it to a customer's next invoice.

## Upload receipts
Attach a receipt as a JPG, PNG or PDF file of up to 10 MB. In the mobile app you can photograph receipts directly.

## Categories
Ledgerly includes common categories such as Travel, Software and Office supplies. You can add your own categories under Settings > Expenses.
