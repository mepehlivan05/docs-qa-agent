# Creating your account

Ledgerly is online invoicing and bookkeeping software for freelancers and small businesses.

## Sign up
Go to ledgerly.example/signup and enter your name, email address and a password of at least 10 characters. You can also sign up with a Google account. Every new account starts on the Free plan; no credit card is required.

## Verify your email
We send a verification email right after sign-up. Click the link within 48 hours. Until your email is verified you can create drafts, but you cannot send invoices to customers.

## Name your workspace
A workspace holds your company details, customers and invoices. Enter your business name, address and logo under Settings > Company. These details appear on every invoice you send.
