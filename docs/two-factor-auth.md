# Two-factor authentication

## Turn on 2FA
Go to Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password. SMS codes are not supported.

## Backup codes
When you turn on 2FA you receive 10 single-use backup codes. Store them safely; each code works once if you lose access to your authenticator app.

## Require 2FA for the team
On the Business plan, the owner can require every team member to use two-factor authentication.
