# Managing customers

## Add a customer
Click Customers > New customer and enter a name and an email address. Address, phone, currency, tax number and payment terms are optional.

## Import from CSV
Go to Customers > Import and upload a CSV file. The file must have a header row; name and email columns are required. Up to 5,000 customers can be imported at once.

## Merge duplicates
Select two customers and click Merge. All invoices and payments move to the customer you keep. Merging cannot be undone.
