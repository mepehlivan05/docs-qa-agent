# Recurring invoices

Recurring invoices are available on the Pro and Business plans.

## Set up a schedule
Open an invoice and choose Make recurring. Pick a frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly), a start date and, optionally, an end date or a number of repetitions.

## Automatic sending
Each new invoice is created on its scheduled date at 9:00 in the workspace's time zone and emailed to the customer automatically. You can switch a schedule to "create as draft" if you want to review invoices before they go out.

## Auto-charge
If the customer has saved a card, you can turn on auto-charge so that each recurring invoice is paid automatically on its issue date.
