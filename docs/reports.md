# Reports

## Available reports
- **Profit and loss**: income minus expenses for any period.
- **Accounts receivable aging**: unpaid invoices grouped by 0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days overdue.
- **Sales tax summary**: tax collected per tax rate.

## Export
Every report can be exported as CSV or PDF.

## Cash or accrual
Reports use accrual accounting by default (income counts when the invoice is issued). You can switch to cash basis (income counts when the payment is received) at the top of each report.
