# Taxes

## Add tax rates
Create tax rates under Settings > Taxes with a name and a percentage, for example "VAT 20%". You can apply up to 3 tax rates to a single line item.

## Tax-inclusive pricing
By default, prices are entered before tax. Turn on Tax-inclusive pricing in Settings > Taxes if your prices already include tax.

## Tax numbers
Add your VAT or sales tax number under Settings > Company to show it on invoices. You can also store a tax number for each customer.
