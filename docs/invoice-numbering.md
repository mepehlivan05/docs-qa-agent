# Invoice numbers

## Default numbering
Invoices are numbered automatically starting at 0001, in the order they are created.

## Custom prefix and starting number
Under Settings > Invoices you can add a prefix such as INV- or 2026- and set the next invoice number. This is useful when you move to Ledgerly from another tool and want to continue your existing sequence.

## Reusing numbers
Invoice numbers must be unique within a workspace. A deleted invoice's number cannot be reused, so your sequence stays auditable.
