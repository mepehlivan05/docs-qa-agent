# Cancelling your subscription

## How to cancel
Only the workspace owner can cancel. Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Your paid features stay active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the workspace then moves to the Free plan.

## What happens to your data
Cancelling does not delete your data. Invoices, customers and reports stay available on the Free plan.

## Deleting the workspace
To delete everything, go to Settings > Company > Delete workspace. Deleted workspaces can be restored by support for 30 days; after that the data is permanently erased. We recommend exporting your data first.
