# Accepting card payments

## Connect Stripe
Card payments are processed by Stripe. Go to Settings > Payments and click Connect Stripe. You can create a new Stripe account or connect an existing one.

## Fees
Stripe charges its standard processing fee, which is 2.9% + 30 cents per successful card payment in the US. Ledgerly adds a 1% fee on the Free plan and no fee on Pro or Business.

## Payouts
Money is paid out to your bank account by Stripe, usually within 2 business days. Payout timing is set in your Stripe dashboard, not in Ledgerly.

## PayPal
You can also connect PayPal under Settings > Payments. Customers then see both options on the invoice page.
