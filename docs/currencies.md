# Multiple currencies

## Invoice in another currency
Each customer can have their own currency. Ledgerly supports 135 currencies; the currency is chosen per customer and can be changed per invoice.

## Base currency and reports
Your workspace has one base currency, set under Settings > Company. Reports convert all amounts to the base currency.

## Exchange rates
Exchange rates are updated once a day from the European Central Bank. The rate on the invoice's issue date is used for reports.
