# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

## Upgrade
Go to Settings > Billing and choose a higher plan. The upgrade takes effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period.

## Downgrade
A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep your current features until then. No refund is issued for the remaining time.

## What happens to extra seats
If you downgrade to a plan with fewer seats than you use, you must remove members before the downgrade takes effect. Otherwise the downgrade is postponed by one billing period.
