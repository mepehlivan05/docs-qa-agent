# Integrations

## Payments
Stripe and PayPal can be connected under Settings > Payments. See the card payments article for details.

## Zapier
The Zapier integration is available on all plans. Triggers include "invoice paid" and "new customer"; actions include "create invoice".

## QuickBooks
The QuickBooks Online integration is available on the Business plan. Invoices, payments and customers sync to QuickBooks every hour. Sync is one-way: changes made in QuickBooks are not sent back to Ledgerly.
